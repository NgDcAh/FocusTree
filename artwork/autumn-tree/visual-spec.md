# Autumn Tree Stage Redesign — Visual Specification

- **Goal:** Build a six-stage autumn tree family for the Pixel Art Gallery, with Stage 6 carrying the reference’s old-growth silhouette and visual richness.
- **Silhouette:** A wide, asymmetric crown with stepped, irregular edges; lobed foliage masses overlap around readable sky gaps. The crown narrows into a twisting, flared trunk and roots, all sharing a consistent bottom anchor.
- **Structure:** A dark central trunk divides into thick, tapered primary limbs and finer secondary branches. Branch tips emerge between leaf masses, with limbs disappearing beneath foliage to establish depth.
- **Foliage:** Distinct scalloped cluster plates, each shaped by offset lobes rather than circles. Use near-black auburn undersides, burnt-orange midtones, and small golden top-left leaf groups. Keep sparse branch windows and a few drifting leaves as intentional accents.
- **Palette and light:** Limited warm ramp from deep umber and burgundy through rust, orange, amber, and pale gold; cool plum-brown shadows. Light comes from upper left, so cluster undersides and right-facing bark stay dark.
- **Hierarchy:** Stage 1 has two leaves; Stage 2 establishes a thin crooked stem; Stage 3 adds visible forked limbs and small clusters; Stages 4–5 build staggered crown layers; Stage 6 reaches the full reference-inspired canopy and twisted trunk.
- **Canvas and alignment:** Detailed stages use a 128×160 logical canvas. All stages share a baseline at y=154 and a centered trunk axis, while crowns widen and branch complexity grows.
- **Acceptance:** Six independently composed sprites, consistent base alignment, crisp intentional clusters at native size, warm depth and a readable branch structure, lint reviewed per sprite, exported sheet and gallery metadata inspected.
